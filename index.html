yt-automation

yt-automation is a private tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel, Pursuit of Suns, to produce science videos for that channel. It runs on the owner's own computer, has no other users and offers no sign-up or public service.

What it does with YouTube API Services

  • Uploads the channel's own videos as private, with their thumbnail and captions, after the owner signs in with Google and grants access. The owner reviews every video in YouTube Studio and decides whether and when to publish it. The tool does not delete videos and does not read, write or moderate comments or ratings.
  • Reads the channel's own analytics (YouTube Analytics and Reporting, read-only) to measure how each episode performs.
  • Reads public data of a short list of science channels and YouTube search results, with an API key, to understand which topics viewers are looking for. This data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.

Uses YouTube API Services. By using this tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.
